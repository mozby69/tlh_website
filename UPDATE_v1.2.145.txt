THE LEISURE HUB v1.2.145 - Food Stall Rental Early Termination

Changes:
- Adds End Rental Early for active Food Stall Rentals that have already started.
- Admin enters Actual End Date, final agreed Stall Rental Amount, and reason.
- Preserves the original scheduled end date for audit history.
- Stores who ended the rental early and when.
- Immediately opens Electricity/Water final billing after early termination.
- Shortens the active rental end date so the stall becomes available for later dates.
- Prevents standard Edit Rental from changing the record after early termination/final billing begins.
- Combined Sales automatically uses the revised stall rental amount plus final utilities.

Database:
The application attempts the v1.2.145 schema update automatically.
If the web database account cannot ALTER TABLE, run:
  database/upgrade_v1.2.145_food_stall_early_end.sql

Install:
Copy these files over the matching paths in your existing TLH v1.2.144 installation.
