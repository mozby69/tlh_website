THE LEISURE HUB v1.3.1 - Simplified Rental Form

This update simplifies Admin > Rentals > New Rental without changing rental accounting or availability rules.

Changes:
- Compact Client row.
- Smaller rentable rows with quantity hidden for exclusive spaces.
- Complimentary / Free moved to a compact inline option.
- Technical billing-mode summary hidden unless selected rentables conflict.
- Compact Base Total / Monthly Base display.
- Schedule section shortened; monthly rentals use Lease Start / Lease End labels automatically.
- Initial Payment remains collapsed until Admin chooses Record payment now.
- Security Deposit and Notes moved under More Options.
- Complimentary rentals hide Initial Payment and keep base charges at P0.00.
- Mobile form stacks cleanly.

No database migration is required.
