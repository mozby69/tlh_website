TLH v1.2.142 - Batch Reservation Permanent Delete Fix

Affected files:
- admin/batch-reservation-delete.php
- admin/batch-reservation-view.php
- admin/reservations.php
- VERSION.txt
- service-worker.js

What changed:
- Paid batch reservations can now be permanently deleted by an Administrator.
- Deletion requires re-entering the currently signed-in Administrator password.
- The confirmation screen shows the batch, connected reservation dates, payment transaction count, and net Sales/Collections impact.
- Confirmed deletion removes all connected reservation dates, their payment ledger rows, batch payment scopes, and batch payment master records.
- The related amounts no longer appear in Sales/Collections reports.
- The whole operation is transactional and rolls back if any step fails.

Database changes: None.
