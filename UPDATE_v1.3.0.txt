THE LEISURE HUB v1.3.0 - Generic Rentals Engine

Major upgrade
-------------
The Food Stall Rental module is now a generic Rentals system. Existing Food Stall
spaces, rentals, payments, flexible dates, extensions, complimentary rentals, and
utility charges are automatically copied into the new generic rental tables.
Legacy tables are preserved for rollback/history and the old Food Stall URLs redirect
to the new Rentals pages.

Admin navigation
----------------
- Rental Calendar remains directly below Booking Calendar.
- Rentals replaces Food Stall Rentals directly below Batch Reservations.
- Venue Management > Manage Rentables configures the catalog.

Rentable catalog
----------------
Admin can add rentables without changing PHP code:
- Food stall spaces
- Restaurant / cafe / kiosk / commercial spaces
- Tents
- Chairs
- Tables
- Other future spaces/items

Each rentable configures:
- Category
- Exclusive or quantity-based availability
- Total quantity (when quantity-based)
- One-time/agreed or monthly recurring billing
- Flexible-date support
- Electricity/Water applicability
- Active/inactive status

Pricing remains manual. No default rental price is stored in the catalog.

Rental transactions
-------------------
- One rental may contain multiple rentables when they use the same billing mode.
- Quantity-based rentables check remaining inventory on every occupied date.
- Exclusive spaces prevent overlapping rentals.
- Continuous and flexible dates are supported when allowed by the rentable.
- Complimentary / free rentals remain supported.
- Payments, extensions, early termination, permanent delete, print, and calendar are supported.
- Additional configurable charge types can be created by Admin.
- Optional Security Deposit requirement with separate receipt/refund history. Deposits do not count as Sales or rental Collections.

Commercial / long-term spaces
-----------------------------
Monthly recurring rentals generate billing periods automatically.
- Monthly rent is manually entered by Admin.
- Electricity and Water can be finalized per billing period.
- Rental extension acts as renewal and adds new monthly billing periods.
- Rentals ending within 30 days display For Renewal.

Reports
-------
- Collections now use the generic Rental payment ledger.
- One-time Rental Sales are recognized by rental start date.
- Monthly Rental Sales are recognized by monthly billing-period date.
- Venue Reservation Sales remain unchanged.

Database
--------
Automatic migration: database/upgrade_v1.3.0_generic_rentals.sql
The migration creates the new generic tables and the application copies existing
Food Stall history into them without deleting the legacy data.

Deployment
----------
Keep your existing config/local.php on the server. Back up the database before the
first v1.3.0 deployment because this is a structural rental-module upgrade.
