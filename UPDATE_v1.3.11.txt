TLH v1.3.11

Calendar Viewer rental details:
- Rental Calendar entries are now clickable for Calendar Viewer accounts.
- Clicking an entry opens a dedicated read-only Rental Details page.
- Viewer details include tenant/contact, organization, schedule, status, rentables, locations, quantities, flexible dates, and notes.
- Financial information is intentionally hidden.
- Edit, payment, extend, cancel, delete, print, and other rental actions remain unavailable to Calendar Viewer accounts.
- Admin rental workflows are unchanged.
