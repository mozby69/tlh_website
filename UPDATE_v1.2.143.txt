THE LEISURE HUB v1.2.143 - Food Stall Final Billing

Apply this package on top of TLH v1.2.142.
Preserve the folder structure and overwrite the matching files.

WHAT CHANGED
- Food Stall Rentals now support post-rental Electricity and Water charges.
- Electricity and Water are manually encoded by Admin only on/after the rental end date.
- Enter 0.00 when a utility was not used.
- Final charges increase the Food Stall Total Due and combined Sales value.
- Existing rental payments are preserved; any final utility charge creates/updates the remaining balance.
- Past open rentals display as "For Final Billing".
- A rental can only be marked Completed after final charges are confirmed and the total balance is fully paid.
- Sales reports now include Stall Rental + Electricity + Water.

DATABASE
The application automatically adds these columns to food_stall_rentals:
- electricity_amount
- water_amount
- final_billing_notes
- final_billed_at
- final_billed_by

No manual SQL import is normally required. If the production DB user cannot ALTER tables,
run database/upgrade_v1.2.143_food_stall_final_billing.sql once.
